Convert Lloyds Bank CSV to QBO / QFX / OFX

These steps are from Lloyds Bank's help for Online for Business, the business service. They are not the personal mobile app. The export formats Lloyds names there are CSV and QIF. Quicken says a QIF file will not import as Web Connect, and QuickBooks Desktop will not import QIF either.

Convert your file now: open the converter. It runs in your browser, your statement is never uploaded, and it's free up to 30 transactions per file.

Export CSV or QIF

View your bank statements says Online for Business may show either a new layout or the previous one.

If you see the new look:

  1. Log in to Online for Business.
  2. Select Statement options.
  3. Go to Export transactions (CSV, QIF).

If you do not see the new look:

  1. Log in to Online for Business.
  2. Select View statement next to the account.
  3. Go to Statement options and Export transactions (CSV, QIF).

Search limits on that page: with the new look, search by date, amount, or type going back up to 12 months. Without the new look, search by date, description, or type over a three-month period. Export the CSV, not the QIF, when you need QuickBooks or a current Quicken Web Connect import.

CSV columns

The source linked below does not publish the column headings in the file. After you download it, use the header row you actually see. In the converter, map a date, a description, and either one signed amount or separate money-in and money-out columns. The preview rewrites dates as YYYY-MM-DD so you can confirm the day and the month before you download.

Convert it

  1. Open the converter and drop in the CSV or Excel file. Excel (.xlsx or .xls) is fine.
  2. If the bank put your name or account number above the table, set the header row to the row that names the columns.
  3. Map the date, the description, and either one signed amount or separate paid-in and paid-out columns. Confirm the dates in the preview, which shows them as YYYY-MM-DD.
  4. Choose QBO for QuickBooks Desktop, QFX for Quicken, or OFX, and download.

Import the converted file

  • QuickBooks Online
  • QuickBooks Desktop (needs a .qbo file)
  • Quicken for Windows or Mac (needs a .qfx file)

FAQ

Is QIF enough for Quicken?

Quicken's Web Connect troubleshooting page says QIF files will not work with Web Connect. Convert the CSV to QFX. See import a QFX file into Quicken.

Does this apply to the Lloyds personal app?

The steps above are the Online for Business article. Lloyds' personal app is not described there.

I can only search three months.

That is the limit Lloyds states for statement search on the layout that is not the new look. The new look's search goes back up to 12 months.
